Reading voice

The most natural-sounding voice on this device is picked automatically. ★ marks the higher-quality ones.
Want a much more natural voice? On iPad or iPhone, open Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English and download a voice marked Premium or Enhanced (Ava, Zoe or Evan sound great). Then come back here and pick it. On a Mac it's System Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › System voice › Manage Voices. In Edge, the “Natural” voices are already the best choice.
